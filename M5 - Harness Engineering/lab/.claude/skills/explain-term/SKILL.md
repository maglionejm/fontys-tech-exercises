# Explaining a term in plain words

The module explains every technical term twice: once precisely, once "in plain words" with one family of car metaphors (engine, steering, brakes, dashboard, GPS, glovebox, trailer hitch, convoy). This procedure keeps explanations consistent with the course.

## Steps

1. Read `docs/glossary.md` and find the entry for the term. If there is no entry, say so and offer the closest entry instead of inventing one.
2. Give the precise definition from the glossary in one sentence.
3. Add one sentence that starts with "In plain words:" and uses the car analogy the glossary gives for that term. Do not introduce a new metaphor family.
4. Finish with `Source: docs/glossary.md`.

## Answer format

Three lines at most: the definition, the plain-words sentence, the source line.
